Need food or shelter now?

Our volunteers deliver recovered meals in the evening in downtown Chicago and Hyde Park. Because we share whatever safe food we recover each day, times and places change.

To learn where our team will be, email info@bridgepantrychicago.org. The services at right are open every day and can help right away.

Students: if you are struggling to afford food, tell us what would help. We are building student support and will connect you with campus and city resources in the meantime. Write to us.

Get help today

Free services. Call, text, or look up what is near you.

  • Shelter: call 311

    The City of Chicago takes shelter requests 24 hours a day through 311. From outside the city, call 312-744-5000.

  • Food: Greater Chicago Food Depository

    Find a food pantry, soup kitchen, shelter meal, or mobile distribution near you at the Food Depository’s find food map, or call 773-247-3663.

  • Everything else: call 2-1-1

    211 Metro Chicago connects you to food, housing, and other help 24/7. You can also text your ZIP code to 898-211 or visit 211metrochicago.org.

In an emergency, call 911.

Bridge Pantry Chicago is a volunteer initiative and is not a crisis service. Hours and availability of the services above can change; please confirm before you travel.
