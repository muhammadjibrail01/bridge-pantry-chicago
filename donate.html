How gifts will be used

Most of every dollar goes to the people and equipment that move food safely from restaurants to neighbors.

Planned allocation of operating costs, based on the 2026 financial model in our research paper for a refrigerated recovery vehicle. Final allocations will be published with our first annual report.

Other ways to help right now

You do not have to wait for giving to open to make a difference.

  • Bring your kitchen on board
