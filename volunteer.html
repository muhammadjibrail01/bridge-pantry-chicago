Become Part of the Bridge

Volunteers power every pickup and every meal. Find a role that fits your skills and your schedule.

Ways to help

Some roles happen on an evening route. Others happen from your laptop. All of them move food from where it is wasted to where it is needed.

  • Food Pickup Coordination

    Schedule and run evening pickups from partner restaurants, keep drivers and partner contacts in sync, and make sure every pickup is logged.

  • Community Distribution

    Sort, pack, and hand out recovered meals the same evening, with respect for the people receiving them.

  • Outreach

    Build relationships with neighbors, campus groups, and community organizations, and share information about benefits, shelter, and food resources.

  • Data and Research

    Track meals, weights, and routes; analyze impact; and support grant reporting and policy research.

  • Operations

    Keep supplies stocked, food-safe packaging ready, and schedules and logs running smoothly.

  • Marketing and Communications

    Tell our story responsibly through writing, design, photography with consent, and social media.

Partner with us

Turn surplus food into community impact. Partnering is free, flexible, and designed around your closing routine.

Who can partner

  • Restaurants
  • Cafes
  • Catering Services
  • Grocers
  • Community Organizations

  • Reduced food waste

    Keep safe, unsold food out of the dumpster and cut the volume you throw away.

  • Community impact

    Know that your surplus feeds a neighbor the same night.

  • Sustainability leadership

    Show customers, staff, and funders that your business acts on waste.

  • Local engagement

    Join a growing network of Chicago businesses and organizations working on food access.

What we ask of you

  • About eight to ten meals, or one tray, per pickup
  • A named staff contact
  • Sealed containers of safe, unsold food. Never customers’ leftovers.
  • A 30 to 45 minute pickup window

What we provide

  • Free pickup by volunteers and community partners
  • A schedule that fits you: daily, several times per week, or weekly
  • Clear, simple written agreements
  • A record of what we collect from your location, on request
